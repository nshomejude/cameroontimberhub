Dark mode

A warm, forest-aligned dark theme. It's class-based (.dark on <html>), respects the OS preference, and is toggled from the app bar (sun / moon). The status-bar tint follows. Components carry dark: variants; on-dark branded panels (the forest covers & CTAs) stay as-is.
