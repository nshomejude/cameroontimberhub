## 1. Executive Commercial Model

Cameroon Timber Hub should monetize the platform through multiple coordinated revenue streams rather than relying on one subscription. The commercial system must support the domestic timber economy, the export trade, and higher-value compliance/data/enterprise services without putting the core knowledge and discovery layer behind a paywall.

| Revenue stream | Primary customer | Launch pricing logic |
|---|---|---|
| Subscriptions | Buyers, suppliers, dealers, exporters | Fixed monthly / annual plans |
| Marketplace transaction fees | Suppliers / buyers using protected trade workflows | Percentage of completed transaction with configurable cap |
| Verification | Suppliers, processors, exporters | Annual fixed fee; approval is evidence-based |
| Compliance | Exporters, buyers, enterprises | Monthly workspace subscription |
| Traceability | Suppliers, exporters, enterprises | Monthly subscription / enterprise contract |
| Market intelligence | Professionals, institutions, enterprises | Monthly subscription + enterprise data licensing |
| Education | Professionals and learners | Free core knowledge; paid courses/certificates |
| Promotion | Suppliers and industry advertisers | Fixed campaign/listing fees |
| Trade services | Buyers, exporters | Quoted or fixed service fees |
| Enterprise/API | Large buyers, exporters, institutions | Negotiated contract with transparent floor pricing |


> **Note:** Commercial objective: Domestic market pricing is deliberately accessible so thousands of local suppliers, dealers, artisans, contractors and buyers can participate; export and enterprise products carry higher ARPU because they deliver deeper workflows, compliance, intelligence and integrations.



## 2. Pricing Principles and Customer Segmentation

- All prices must be visible before purchase. No mandatory platform fee may be introduced at checkout unless it is displayed in the applicable price breakdown.

- Free education, core species knowledge, basic legal resources and basic supplier discovery remain open to maximize search authority and marketplace reach.

- Subscription status, verification status, certification status and trust/ranking status are separate concepts. Paying for a plan does not automatically create a verification badge.

- Domestic customers are billed primarily in XAF. International customers are billed primarily in USD. EUR display may be supported, but settlement must retain the contracted transaction currency.

- Every price is versioned with effective-from and effective-until dates. Historical invoices never change because a price changes later.

- Enterprise pricing may be negotiated, but every enterprise contract must record the negotiated rates, minimums, discounts, term, renewal, and fee caps in a signed commercial record.

- All percentages are disclosed before an order is committed, including marketplace commission, payment-provider charges where passed through, taxes, delivery and optional services.

## 15. Transaction and Trade-Service Fees

| Customer / supplier plan | Domestic marketplace fee | International marketplace fee | Maximum fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free / unlisted | 3.0% | 5.0% | $5,000 / 5% whichever is lower |
| Starter / Professional | 2.5% | 4.0% | $5,000 / 4% whichever is lower |
| Pro / Business | 2.0% | 3.0% | $5,000 / 3% whichever is lower |
| Enterprise | Negotiated | Negotiated | Contractual cap |


- Commission is assessed on the transaction value defined in the order record. Shipping, taxes and pass-through charges are excluded unless the commercial contract explicitly says otherwise.

- No marketplace commission is charged on a transaction that is cancelled before supplier acceptance, unless a separate paid service was already consumed.

- When a protected transaction is refunded, the commission treatment follows the configured refund policy and is explicitly shown on the credit/refund record.

- CT Hub must not claim to hold customer funds in escrow unless the required payment, licensing and safeguarding structure exists.

> **Implementation note (2026-10-01).** Rates live in `commission_rules` (Admin → Commission rules), stored as fractions, seeded by `CommissionRuleSeeder` / data migration `2026_10_01_170010` (insert-if-missing, never overwrites). Tier mapping onto the real plan slugs: *Free / unlisted* → sell/`free` (a supplier with no plan is rated as sell/`free`); *Starter / Professional* → sell/`professional` and export/`exporter-professional`; *Pro / Business* → export/`exporter-business` (the sell segment has no business tier); *Enterprise* → no seeded rule (0% until a negotiated rule is added). Deal segment (owner approved 2026-10-01, data migration `2026_10_01_180020`): `dealer-free` = Free / unlisted rates, `dealer-pro` = Starter / Professional, `dealer-network` = Pro / Business. The "$5,000" cap is stored as `cap_amount` 5000 + `cap_currency` USD and converted into the order currency via `config('timber.commission.usd_to_xaf')` (default 600; EUR via the XAF/EUR peg); with no rate for a currency only the percent cap applies. **Owner decision:** commission is charged on EVERY platform order (no Trade Assurance requirement), once, when the supplier confirms it (awarded → confirmed) and snapshotted on `orders.commission_*` — so a cancellation before supplier acceptance is never charged; a cancellation after confirmation (only possible before shipping) credits it back in full; a dispute decision can credit part of it (`commission_credit` activity log). Pre-commit disclosure: buyer quote screen (as supplier-paid, not added to the buyer's total) and the supplier API (`commission_preview` on quotes, `commission` on orders). See RUNBOOK → Marketplace commission.

> **Implementation note — collection (owner decision 2026-10-01).** Commission is **collected from suppliers by manual deposit** (MTN MoMo / Orange Money transfer to the platform's number, or bank deposit / transfer) — there is no automatic debit. `commission:issue-statements` (1st of each month, for the previous month; `--month=YYYY-MM` to re-run) issues one hash-chained `commission_statements` row per supplier company per currency (`CTH-CS-YYYY-NNNNN`), listing every order whose commission was charged that month (`orders.commission_charged_at`) at its net amount (charged − credited); an order is billed once (`commission_statement_lines.order_id` unique among non-void charge lines) and a credit issued after billing becomes a negative adjustment on the next statement, so an issued statement never changes. Zero / net-credit totals are not issued. Due after `timber.commission.statement_due_days` (15) days; status issued → partially_paid → paid, overdue once past due, void by finance. Suppliers report deposits (exporter panel → Commission, `POST /api/v1/supplier/commission/statements/{number}/deposits`) against the platform payment instructions (`commission_payment_settings`, Admin → Commission payment instructions); finance confirms (amount received) or rejects them (Admin → Commission deposits, `payments.manage`), or records a deposit received without a report. Enforcement `timber.commission.block_on_overdue_days` (default 15 days past due, owner decision; `off` disables) blocks new quotes (409 `commission_overdue`). See RUNBOOK → Commission collection.

## 20. Taxes, Currencies and Payment Rules

| Rule | Specification |
|---|---|
| Domestic display currency | XAF / FCFA |
| International display currency | USD |
| Supported display currency | EUR may be supported as a display/checkout currency where payment rails support it |

## 23. Pricing Governance and Audit Controls

1. All price changes require an authenticated administrator with the Pricing Manager permission or equivalent.

1. Every price version stores old value, new value, effective dates, currency, reason, creator, approver and affected products/plans.

1. Commission changes are versioned exactly like subscription prices.

1. Customer invoices store a frozen copy of all rates applied to that invoice.

1. Enterprise discounts require approval according to discount thresholds.

1. No staff member may manually alter an already-issued invoice without a credit/debit-note workflow.

1. Every manual credit, refund, fee waiver or commission override is audit logged.

1. Pricing APIs are read-only for customer clients; mutation operations are admin-only and authorization controlled.

## 25. Revenue Model and Management KPIs

## Implementation notes

### Payment-provider fees (§19 / §20 "Payment fees")

- **Configuration** — per provider in `config/payments.php` (`fee_percent` as a percent, `fee_fixed` in the provider's currency, `fee_bearer` = `buyer` | `platform`), env-backed (`PAYPAL_FEE_*`, `STRIPE_FEE_*`, `MTN_MOMO_FEE_*`, `ORANGE_MONEY_FEE_*`). Admin overrides live on the provider's `payment_settings` row (Admin → Payment settings → *Edit fees*, `payments.manage`, activity-logged) and win over env column by column. Launch defaults: PayPal 4.4% + 0.30 USD borne by the **buyer**; Stripe 3.4% + 0.30 borne by the platform; MTN MoMo / Orange Money 0, platform.
- **Math** — `App\Services\Payments\ProviderFeeCalculator` (pure, bcmath strings; XAF/XOF 0dp, others 2dp). Buyer-borne: `total = (price + fixed) / (1 − percent/100)` rounded **up**, `fee = total − price`, so the platform always nets the list price (e.g. $29 → fee $1.65, total $30.65). Platform-borne: `total = price`, `fee = price × percent + fixed` (half-up), recorded as a platform cost.
- **Disclosure before authorisation** — the web checkout (`/billing/checkout/{plan}`) shows *Subtotal · {Provider} processing fee · Total* for the selected method when a fee is passed through (PayPal), and a per-method note visible without JavaScript; mobile money shows the plain price. The mobile app gets the same numbers from `GET /api/v1/billing/checkout/{plan}`.
- **Persistence** — each `payments` row snapshots `base_amount` (price incl. any tax — what the platform is owed), `provider_fee_amount`, `provider_fee_bearer`; `amount` stays exactly what the payer is charged, is what PayPal's order is created for, and is what the captured amount is verified against. Revenue-share maths (referral commission) uses `Payment::baseAmount()` / `subtotalAmount()` (pre-tax), never `amount`.
- **Documents & reporting** — the subscription invoice carries the fee as its own untaxed line (lines = plan subtotal + fee; subtotal + tax = total = amount charged); the success page and `/billing` payment history show it; the receipt amount is the total paid. Admin → Commission report → *Payment provider fees* shows fees collected from buyers vs absorbed by the platform, per provider and currency (never summed across currencies).
- **Not converted** — a fixed fee in one currency is never FX-converted: a charge in a different currency than the provider's fee currency gets the percentage only (unreachable today: USD plans → PayPal, XAF plans → mobile money).
